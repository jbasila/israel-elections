Eight elections, 2013–2026

Turnout measured from the Central Elections Committee's results for each ballot box. Sector turnout counts only voters at their home station, so double-envelope voters (mostly soldiers) are left out and the Jewish figure is understated.

How sectors are drawn

Arab: localities the Central Bureau of Statistics classifies as non-Jewish or Bedouin, plus ballot boxes in mixed cities where Arab-led lists won at least 50%. Druze: 16 Druze-majority localities. Haredi: ballot boxes where UTJ, Shas and Haredi Public won at least 75%. This is a conservative core that captures about 45% of Haredi-list votes; turnout comes out at 74% at any cutoff between 50% and 90%. Jewish (other): everything else. The model validates exactly against the official result. Code and data: github.com/jbasila/israel-elections.
